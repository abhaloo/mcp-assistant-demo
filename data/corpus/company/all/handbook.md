# Bloomfield Print Works — Employee Handbook (Demo)

Welcome to **Bloomfield Print Works**, a fictional commercial print shop used for this demo.

## Paid time off

Full-time staff accrue **18 days** of paid time off per year. Submit PTO requests at least **5 business days** before the start date.

## Customer service

When a customer asks about invoice status, confirm the invoice number and billing period before quoting balances. Never share another customer's account details.

## Collection rate (finance)

**Collection rate** is cash received divided by invoiced amount for a period. Finance uses it to track how much billed work was actually collected in TZS.

## Safety

Report spills of ink or solvent to the floor supervisor immediately. Wear gloves when handling press chemicals.
